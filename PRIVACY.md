# Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति: Daily Dose of Gita

Last updated: 14 October 2026

## English
**Daily Dose of Gita** is an offline app for reading the Bhagavad Gita and finding shlokas for how you feel.

- **We do not collect any personal data.** The app has no account, no login and no ads.
- **No data leaves your phone.** The Gita text and mood guidance are stored inside the app. Your language choice and daily-notification time are saved only on your device.
- **No internet connection is used** by the app.
- **Notifications:** If you turn them on, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a daily shloka on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
- **No third parties:** We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- **Children:** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.
- **Not professional advice:** The app offers guidance from the Gita. It is not a substitute for a doctor, counsellor or other professional help. If you are in distress in India, you can call Tele-MANAS at 14416 (free, 24x7).
